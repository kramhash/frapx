## Baked SDF skull

<ShowcaseVideo
  src="/frapx/showcase/sdf-skull"
  poster="/frapx/showcase/sdf-skull.jpg"
  label="A white skull turning in the dark: orbiting spheres melt into it and pull away, then spines grow out of it as it rounds into a sea urchin, and it turns back into the skull."
/>

A skull baked from a mesh into a 3D signed distance texture, then treated in
the shader like any other distance function. Orbiting spheres smooth-union into
it and pull away; then the skull blends into an analytic sphere while spines —
cones placed on rings of latitude — grow out of its surface until it is a sea
urchin, and everything runs back. None of this needs the mesh: the volume is
sampled with `frapx_sdVolume` from
[`@frapx/glsl-sdf-volume`](/frapx/packages/glsl-sdf-volume/), blended with
[`@frapx/glsl-sdf`](/frapx/packages/glsl-sdf/) operators and marched with
[`@frapx/glsl-raymarch`](/frapx/packages/glsl-raymarch/). The 12-second loop is
a pure function of time, so the live page and the clip are the same frames.

- **Live:** [/frapx/demos/sdf-skull/](/frapx/demos/sdf-skull/) (WebGL2; the
  pointer nudges the camera)
- **Packages:** [`@frapx/fx-canvas`](/frapx/packages/fx-canvas/),
  [`@frapx/glsl-sdf-volume`](/frapx/packages/glsl-sdf-volume/),
  [`@frapx/glsl-sdf`](/frapx/packages/glsl-sdf/),
  [`@frapx/glsl-raymarch`](/frapx/packages/glsl-raymarch/) and
  [`@frapx/tool-render`](/frapx/packages/tool-render/)
- **Source:** [`examples/vite-sdf-skull`](https://github.com/kramhash/frapx/tree/main/examples/vite-sdf-skull)
  — its README has the bake and render steps.
- **Model:** [ScatteringSkull](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/ScatteringSkull)
  by Vladimir Petkovic (CC0), baked to `r16f` by the 3dsdf-texture Blender
  extension.

The clip above is a muted 720p, 60 fps render of the page.

## LIMINAL VEIN

<ShowcaseVideo
  src="/frapx/showcase/liminal-vein"
  poster="/frapx/showcase/liminal-vein.jpg"
  label="LIMINAL VEIN: a camera drifting through a dark tunnel of ribs lit by glowing rings, which turn from circles to hexagons to stars on the beat."
/>

A seamless 15-second loop, set to its own song, through a tunnel of ribs lit
only by a few glowing rings. The tunnel is a raymarched signed distance field
drawn as an [`@frapx/fx-passes`](/frapx/packages/fx-passes/) scene pass that
accumulates samples, so every frame is anti-aliased and motion blurred; bloom
and the display pass (vignette, tone curve, dither) run once per frame on the
mean. The song drives the uniforms — the kick's flash, the hats, each bar's
loudness and the cross-section turning from circle to hexagon to star — as a
pure function of time through `bindUniforms()`, so a frame looks the same
however it is reached. That is what lets
[`@frapx/tool-render`](/frapx/packages/tool-render/) render the same page frame
by frame at 1920×1080, 60 fps, with the song.

- **Packages:** [`@frapx/fx-passes`](/frapx/packages/fx-passes/),
  [`@frapx/glsl-sdf`](/frapx/packages/glsl-sdf/),
  [`@frapx/glsl-raymarch`](/frapx/packages/glsl-raymarch/),
  [`@frapx/tool-capture`](/frapx/packages/tool-capture/) and
  [`@frapx/tool-render`](/frapx/packages/tool-render/)
- **Source:** [`examples/vite-liminal-vein`](https://github.com/kramhash/frapx/tree/main/examples/vite-liminal-vein)
  — the page plays live with the song (WebGL2 with `EXT_color_buffer_float`),
  and its README has the render steps.

The clip above is a muted 720p cut of that render.
